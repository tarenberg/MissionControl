# Art Shipping Best Practices: Avoiding Disasters

When having an artwork shipped, following specialist recommendations can help avoid common mishaps.

## Disaster 1: Wrong Address Delivery
**ARTSY TIP: GET THE TRACKING NUMBER**
- Use tracking numbers to monitor transit and estimate arrival.
- Ask galleries or auction houses for the tracking number in advance.
- For small shippers without tracking, request contact info: company name, phone, and email.

## Disaster 2: Damage in Transit
**ARTSY TIP: INVEST IN INSURANCE**
- Standard practice for high-value items.
- Costs vary by worth, weight, and size.
- Double-check that the gallery/auction house has included it.
- Essential for reimbursement if damage occurs.

## Disaster 3: Left in the Rain
**ARTSY TIP: CHOOSE SIGNATURE-REQUIRED SHIPPING**
- Ensures artwork arrives in safe hands, not left on a doorstep.
- Alternative: Ship to an office or doorman building for convenience and safety.

## Disaster 4: Prohibitive Shipping Costs
**ARTSY TIP: HOLD OFF ON FRAMING**
- Framed works (especially glass) are expensive to ship due to heavy wooden crates.
- For works on paper/prints, consider shipping unframed in a tube to a local framer.
- Consolidate: Ask if the gallery can include your work in a shipment for a nearby art fair or exhibition.
- International: Waiting a little longer can sometimes lower fees for overseas transport.

## Final Word
Shipping is not the place to cut corners. Investing in extra precautions ensures your artwork arrives safely. Refer to the seller (galleries/auction houses) for expert guidance on logistics.
