9/16/2012 Sunday Picnic in the Park

Woke up this morning all crippled up with back pain so I took the bike out to try and loosen it up. Took my usual route through the Yale campus and up Orange street to Edgerton Park where they were having their annual Picnic in the Park. Grabbed a couple of cold burnt hamburgers for lunch and listened to some big band music. I found an empty bench where I sat and did some sketching. An old gentlemen was helped by his daughter to the bench next to me. He's an ex-professor at Yale who loves to play piano with his wife. I asked him if they had two pianos and he said they had two grand pianos, so I asked him if they did dueling pianos. He thought that was hilarious and asked if he could use that line. I told him yes but he should make sure she always wins. On my way home I cruised through the square by the Bineike manuscript library. The back door to Woolsie Hall was opened so I stopped to check it out. They were doing auditions of classic Chinese performers so I took a seat in the back and got to see traditional dancers, classic stringed instruments and acrobats. Outside there was one of the performers playing classic Russian folk songs on an ErHu, a two stringed instrument with a bow. Talk about cultural diversity!
