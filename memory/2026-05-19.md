- **Gated Transmission Bug (v7.2)**: Resolved the voice capture failure ("not hearing me"). Identified two critical logic issues:
  1. Gating the *actual WebSocket transmission* with an RMS threshold of `0.005` was cutting off his voice if his microphone gain was low or if he spoke quietly. Gemini was receiving zero audio data.
  2. The silence timer trigger was mistakenly nested inside the "active speaking" block, causing it to fail to transition back when the voice volume dropped below the threshold.

  * **Always send audio**: Removed the gate for audio transmission. The microphone stream is now continuously piped directly to Gemini once connected (allowing Gemini's native cloud-side noise suppression and VAD to process the full signal).
  * **UI-Only Threshold**: Lowered the UI speaking threshold to a highly sensitive `0.002`. The silence timer is now triggered correctly on the falling edge of speaking, allowing the Orb to transition seamlessly between states.
